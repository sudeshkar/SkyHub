# Connecting in Dubai

Search offers direct flights and journeys with one connection in Dubai, at Dubai International (DXB) or Al Maktoum International (DWC).

## Minimum connection time

A connection is only offered if there is enough time between landing and the next departure:

- Changing planes at DXB needs at least 75 minutes.
- Changing planes at DWC needs at least 60 minutes.

## Changing airport

Some connections land at one Dubai airport and leave from the other. These are shown clearly in the search results. The connection then allows 120 minutes for the transfer between the airports, plus the minimum connection time of the airport you leave from.

## The longest layover

Search does not offer a connection with a layover longer than 12 hours.

## Delays

Search uses the expected times, so if a flight is delayed the connection times follow the new time. A connection that a delay makes too short is no longer offered. A cancelled flight is never offered.

If a flight you have already booked is delayed or cancelled, see "Delays and cancelled flights".
