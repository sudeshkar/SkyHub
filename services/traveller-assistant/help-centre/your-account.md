# Your SkyHub account

You do not need an account to book or manage a booking. An account lists your bookings in one place and remembers passengers' names.

## Creating an account or resetting a password

Enter your email address. We send you a link to choose a password. The same form creates an account or resets a password, and it always says the same thing, so it never reveals whether an address has an account.

- The link works once, and for 1 hour. Asking again replaces the old link.
- A password needs at least 12 characters.
- Until you choose a new password, your old one keeps working.

## Signing in

After 5 wrong passwords in a row, the account is locked for 15 minutes. Choosing a new password with an emailed link ends the lock.

## Your bookings

"Your bookings" lists the bookings made with your account's email address, upcoming and past. Open one to manage it, without typing the reference.

## Saved passengers

You can keep up to 10 names on your account and fill in passengers from them when you book. Only first and last names are kept.

## Changing your email address

Enter the new address and your current password. We send a link to the new address; the address changes when you open it while signed in. After the change, "Your bookings" lists the bookings made with the new address.

We also tell your old address, with the new one partly hidden. That email has a link that works for 7 days: if you did not make the change, it gives the account back to the old address, removes the password, signs out every session and sends you a link to choose a new password.

## Signing out everywhere

"Sign out everywhere" on the account page ends every session of your account on every device. Changing your password or email address does this too.
