# Managing a booking

## Finding your booking

On the **Manage booking** page, enter the booking reference and the last name of any passenger on the booking. Upper and lower case in the last name do not matter.

If you have an account, bookings made with your account's email address are also listed under "Your bookings", and you can open them from there. See "Your SkyHub account".

## How long a session lasts

Once you have opened a booking, the session stays open for 15 minutes. After that, enter the reference and last name again.

**Signing out of a booking** ends every open session of that booking, on every device. Use it on a shared computer. Cancelling a booking does not sign you out, so you can still see the refund.

## What you can do

- **See your flights and seats,** and whether each flight is delayed or cancelled.
- **Change seats.** Pick a new seat for any passenger on any flight. A passenger who has checked in for a flight keeps their seat on that flight; check that passenger out first to change it. See "Checking in online".
- **Check in and get boarding passes.** See "Checking in online".
- **Cancel the booking.** See "Cancellations and refunds".

You cannot change the flights or the passengers' names on a booking. To travel on different flights, cancel and book again; whether you get your money back depends on the refund rules.
