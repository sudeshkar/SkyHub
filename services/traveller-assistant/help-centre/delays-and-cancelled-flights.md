# Delays and cancelled flights

## How you are told

If a flight on your booking is delayed or cancelled, we email you, and the Manage booking page shows it. If the delay or cancellation is withdrawn later, we email you again: for a delay, the original time applies again; for a cancellation, the flight is operating.

## A delayed flight

Your booking stays on the flight. Check-in, boarding and the refund rules all follow the new departure time. A delay on its own does not give a refund. See "Cancellations and refunds".

## A cancelled flight

You have two choices:

- **Cancel your booking for a full refund.** When SkyHub cancels a flight on your booking, cancelling gives you a full refund whenever you do it.
- **Wait to be moved.** SkyHub staff may move your booking to another flight. See below.

## Being moved to another flight

When a flight is disrupted, staff may move bookings to another flight. A person at SkyHub approves every move; nothing moves your booking automatically. A move always:

- keeps the same route, and the new flight does not leave earlier than your original one was due to;
- keeps your connections working, if you have any;
- moves everyone on the booking together, with seats kept together where possible.

A booking is not moved once a passenger has boarded the flight.

You get an email with your new flight and seats. If you had checked in, the move checks you out: your old boarding passes stop working and you need to check in again for the new flight. The email says so.

Being moved does not take away your refund. If you were moved off a flight that SkyHub cancelled, you still get a full refund if you later cancel the booking.
