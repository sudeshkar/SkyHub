# Boarding

## When boarding happens

Boarding normally opens 45 minutes before departure and closes 10 minutes before departure. Some routes have their own boarding times. If the flight is delayed, boarding moves with the new departure time.

Come to the gate in time. Once boarding has closed, the gate cannot board you; staff at the desk decide whether a late passenger can still board.

## At the gate

Staff scan the QR code on your boarding pass. A pass is accepted only if:

- it is a pass SkyHub issued, unchanged;
- it is the latest pass for that passenger and flight (a pass from before you checked out, or from before staff moved your booking, no longer works);
- the booking and the flight are not cancelled;
- the passenger has not already boarded.

Each passenger can board once. Have each passenger's own pass ready.

## If something is wrong

If your pass is refused, go to the desk. Staff can see why and help. Staff can also correct a scan made by mistake.
