# Checking in online

## When you can check in

Online check-in for a flight opens 24 hours before departure and closes 60 minutes before departure. You check in on the Manage booking page, separately for each flight of your booking.

If the flight is delayed, check-in follows the new departure time. A cancelled flight cannot be checked in.

We email you when check-in opens for your flight.

## One passenger at a time, or everyone

You can check in everyone on the booking at once, or one passenger at a time. Each passenger who is checked in gets a boarding pass for that flight.

## Boarding passes

A boarding pass shows the passenger's name, the flight, the seat and a boarding number, with a QR code that staff scan at the gate. Every pass is signed, so a changed pass is refused at the gate.

After check-in you also get an email with the boarding passes attached.

## Seats after check-in

Once a passenger has checked in for a flight, their seat on that flight is fixed. Other passengers on the booking can still change seats until they check in.

## Checking out

You can check out of a flight, one passenger or everyone, until check-in closes. You might do this to change a seat. Checking out cancels the old boarding passes: they no longer work at the gate. If you check in again you get new passes with new boarding numbers.

If your flight is cancelled, you can always check out.

## If staff move you to another flight

If your flight is disrupted and staff move your booking to another flight, you are checked out of the old flight and your old boarding passes stop working. You need to check in again for the new flight. The email about the move tells you this. See "Delays and cancelled flights".
