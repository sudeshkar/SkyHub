# Cancellations and refunds

You cancel a booking yourself on the Manage booking page. Cancelling covers the whole booking: every passenger and every flight. It cannot be undone.

## Do I get my money back?

You get a **full refund** if either of these is true:

- **SkyHub cancelled a flight on your booking.** You get a full refund whenever you cancel. This still holds if staff moved you off the cancelled flight onto another one.
- **You cancel 24 hours or more before the first flight of the booking leaves.** If that flight is delayed, the time is counted from its new departure time.

Otherwise (less than that before the first flight, or after it has left) there is **no refund**. A delay on its own does not give a refund.

## What the refund status means

After you cancel, the booking shows one of these:

- **Refunded:** the money has been returned to the payment method you used.
- **Pending:** the booking is cancelled and the refund is owed, but it has not gone through yet. SkyHub keeps trying automatically and emails you when it is paid. Pressing Cancel again also retries it, and never refunds twice.
- **Not refundable:** the refund rules above did not give a refund.

## Emails

You get an email when you cancel, saying what will be refunded. If the refund was pending and SkyHub's automatic retry pays it, you get another email when it is paid.

## If a cancelled flight is put back

If SkyHub cancels a flight and then puts it back on, a booking you have already cancelled stays cancelled and keeps its refund.
