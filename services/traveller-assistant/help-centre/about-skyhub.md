# About SkyHub

SkyHub is a demonstration airline. It is a portfolio project, not a real airline: its flights are fictional, nobody can travel on them, and no real money is ever taken. Payment uses test methods only, and the e-ticket attached to a confirmation says it is not valid for travel.

## Where SkyHub flies from

SkyHub has two hubs in Dubai:

- **Dubai International (DXB)**
- **Al Maktoum International (DWC)**

You can search for flights between the hubs and their destinations, including connections that change airport in Dubai. See "Connecting in Dubai".

## What the assistant can help with

- Explaining SkyHub's rules: booking, seats, cancellations and refunds, check-in, boarding, delays and cancelled flights, and accounts.
- Pointing you to the right page on the website.

## What the assistant does not do

- It does not cancel a booking, change a seat or check you in. You do those yourself on the Manage booking page, so nothing happens to your booking without you.
- It does not guess. If the help centre does not answer your question, the assistant says so.
- It never asks for a card number or a password. If anything asks you for one in a chat, do not give it.

## Your personal data

Names and contact details are stored encrypted. Staff tools that plan for flight disruptions work without seeing passengers' names or contact details.
