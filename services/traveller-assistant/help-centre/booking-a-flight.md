# Booking a flight

## One booking for the whole trip

A single booking can hold up to 9 passengers and up to 4 flights. Every passenger travels on every flight of the booking, and each passenger has one seat on each flight. The same flight cannot appear twice in one booking. You do not need an account to book.

## Cabins

SkyHub flights have two cabins: **Business class** and **Economy class**. You choose the cabin when you choose seats, from the seat map.

## Choosing seats

After you choose your flights, you pick a seat for each passenger on each flight from the live seat map. When you continue, the seats you chose are held for 10 minutes while you enter passenger details and pay. The page shows a countdown.

- A held seat is shown as taken to everyone else, so nobody can book it while you finish.
- The hold covers all your seats or none of them. If any seat has just been taken, nothing is held and you can choose again.
- If the time runs out, the seats are released and you need to choose seats again.

## Paying

SkyHub works out the price itself; the price shown at the end is the price charged. You are charged first and the booking is made straight after. If the booking cannot be made after you were charged, the charge is refunded automatically.

Pressing the button twice, or retrying after a lost connection, never books or charges you twice.

SkyHub is a demonstration: payment uses test methods and no real card is charged. See "About SkyHub".

## After booking

You get a booking reference on the confirmation page and by email. The email is an itinerary with each flight, its airports and times, and your seats, and it has an e-ticket attached as a PDF. Keep the booking reference: with it and a passenger's last name you can manage the booking. See "Managing a booking".

A cancelled flight is never offered and cannot be booked, so you are never charged for a flight that will not operate.
